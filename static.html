Frequently Asked Questions

What size parts can you scan?

We can handle parts from small components up to approximately half the size of a car. If you're unsure whether your part is too large, send us photos and we'll let you know.

What materials work best?

Matte, non-reflective surfaces scan best. Shiny metal, transparent, or very dark black parts may require special preparation (like temporary coating spray). We'll advise you after reviewing your photos.

Do you offer CAD conversion or reverse engineering?

No, we focus exclusively on providing high-quality scan data (STL and PLY files). CAD conversion requires understanding design intent, which varies by application. You'll receive clean scan data that you can use for your specific needs.

What if my part gets damaged in shipping?

We handle all parts with care, but shipping carries inherent risks. We strongly recommend you purchase shipping insurance for valuable or irreplaceable items. You are responsible for properly packaging your part and insuring shipments both ways.

Are the scan files ready to 3D print?

The scan files we provide are high-quality meshes, but they may require some cleanup or modification depending on your intended use. Most 3D printing slicers can import STL files directly, though you may need to repair or optimize the mesh for your specific application.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we only accept shipments within the United States. International service may be added in the future.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards and PayPal. Payment is split into two parts: 50% deposit before you ship your part, and 50% final payment after you approve the scan screenshots.
