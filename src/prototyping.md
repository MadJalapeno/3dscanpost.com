# Prototyping Services

We scan parts, we don't manufacture them. For complex assemblies we may print a part as a sanity check and send it to you when we return your parts.

Once you have a scan of an existing part, a manufacturing partner can turn it into a physical copy, a modified version or a small batch. This page lists the services we point customers to.

## How scan to prototype works

1. **We [scan your part](/#how-it-works)** and deliver STL and PLY files. These are accurate to ±0.2mm in laser mode.
2. **Optional: [reverse engineering](/reverse-engineering-3d-scanning/).** We recreate the part as a STEP file in SolidWorks, which is the format most CNC and machine shops want.
3. **Upload the file to a manufacturer** for a quote and production.

## Which file do I need?

- **STL** suits 3D printing. See [3D scanning for 3D printing](/3d-scanning-for-3d-printing/).
- **STEP** suits CNC machining, sheet metal and injection mold quoting. A raw scan mesh isn't machinable, so this usually needs reverse engineering first.
