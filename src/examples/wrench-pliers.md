## What it is

My favorite wrench pliers, scanned as a mesh. I actually have the 5", 10" and 16" sizes, all bought from <a href="https://kctool.com/products/knipex-7-pliers-wrench-chrome-plastic-grip" target="_blank" rel="noopener">KC Tool<span class="sr-only"> (external site, opens in a new tab)</span><span aria-hidden="true" title="External link, opens in a new tab"> ↗</span></a>.

## Why I scanned it

I wanted to make a Gridfinity tray to hold my smallest pliers, so I scanned them in and traced the outline to make the tray.

## What the scan shows

The long handles, the sliding jaw and the serrated gripping faces all come through, which makes it a good test of how a scan handles a part with moving pieces and fine teeth. You can even see the Knipex logo on the side.
