# 3D Scanning for 3D Printing

Need to reproduce a broken part, make an adapter that fits an existing object, or print a copy of something that has no CAD model? A 3D scan gives you a digital mesh of the real thing, which you can load into a slicer or modelling tool.

## From physical part to STL

1. **Send photos and a description** of your part: several angles, approximate dimensions and material.
2. **We quote and recommend a scan type.** Laser scanning suits precision parts; infrared scanning suits organic or colored objects. See the [comparison](/#scan-types).
3. **Ship your part**, and include a return shipping label in the package.
4. **We scan and send screenshots** for your approval.
5. **You pay by Venmo or PayPal** and receive your STL and PLY files, and we ship your original part back.

## STL or PLY or STEP?

- <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/STL_(file_format)" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>STL</strong><span class="sr-only"> (Wikipedia, opens in a new tab)</span><span aria-hidden="true" title="External link, opens in a new tab"> ↗</span></a> is the format nearly every slicer and CAD package accepts. It stores triangulated geometry only.
- <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PLY_(file_format)" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>PLY</strong><span class="sr-only"> (Wikipedia, opens in a new tab)</span><span aria-hidden="true" title="External link, opens in a new tab"> ↗</span></a> can carry color information as well as triangulated geometry, which matters if you're printing in color or want a realistic visual of the object.
- <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_10303-21" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>STEP</strong><span class="sr-only"> (Wikipedia, opens in a new tab)</span><span aria-hidden="true" title="External link, opens in a new tab"> ↗</span></a> mathematically models surfaces and is the most accurate format.

We can deliver either. Once you have the file, our [prototyping page](/prototyping/) lists places to get it printed or machined.

## Are scans accurate?

Our scanner has an accuracy of ±0.2mm, and we can use our [STL alignment tool](/tools/3d-mesh-tools/) to scale and align scans as needed. STL files are made of tiny triangles, so surfaces are tessellated. If greater accuracy is needed our [Reverse Engineering Service](/reverse-engineering-3d-scanning/) will make a more accurate CAD model using the scan data as an underlay, and physically measuring the part with calipers, micrometers and thread gauges. This way we can deliver a STEP file, which can be used for CNC machining parts.

## Are scans print-ready?

Scan files are high-quality meshes, but depending on your use they may need cleanup, hole filling or optimization before printing. Most slicers import STL directly. If you need a part that matches existing geometry precisely, our laser mode has ±0.2mm accuracy, and our [reverse engineering](/reverse-engineering-3d-scanning/) service can recreate the part as a STEP file in Solidworks. 

## Tricky surfaces

Shiny, transparent or very dark parts can be hard to scan. We can apply a temporary scanning spray that evaporates after scanning.
